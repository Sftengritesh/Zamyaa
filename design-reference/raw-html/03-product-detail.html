Maison Specification

The Architecture of the Garment

The Gulabi Rose Co-Ord Set reimagines classical Indian regal attire through an uncompromisingly modern minimalist lens. Featuring an elongated, softly structured band-collar tunic with asymmetric side slits, tailored specifically to cascade over high-waisted cigarette trousers.

Top Specifications
  • Mandarin high-notch collar with hidden pearl snaps
  • Deep front overlap slit with delicate hand-couched zari
  • 3/4th tailored sleeves accented with pure silk thread resham
  • Fully lined in breathable, lightweight organic mulmul
Trouser Details
  • Clean flat front waistband with discrete side zip closure
  • Gentle elastication at back waist for dynamic ease
  • Concealed seam pockets deep enough for essential luxury essentials
  • Tapered ankle finish with subtle scalloped hemline

Woven entirely on heritage pit looms by multi-generational master weavers in Madhya Pradesh, the Chanderi silk base provides a subtle crystalline luster and lightweight ethereal drape.

eco Natural Azo-Free Dyes

The striking fuchsia rose hue is achieved through certified non-toxic mineral dyes that protect the integrity of the natural silk proteins.

• Strictly professional dry clean only.
