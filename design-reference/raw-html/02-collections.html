ATELIER DISCOURSE

The Poetry of Color

In Noor-e-Rang, color is neither decoration nor trend—it is inherited alchemy. We returned to the 18th-century dye manifests preserved in the royal archives of the City Palace of Jaipur.

Every garment begins with unbleached mulberry silk spun on pedal looms in Maheshwar. Over forty-five master craftspeople (karigars) work between Bagru’s mud-resist printers and the embroidery ateliers of Sanganer, pinning hand-beaten gold tilla and antique gota ribbons into undulating geometric ripples.

"We listen to the silk tension as the metallic thread enters. If the warp breathes, the color will sing in direct sunlight."
